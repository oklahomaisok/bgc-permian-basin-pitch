  • 6-Month Social Media Content Calendar — Monthly themes tied to fundraising and programs

Impact Infrastructure

Middle of Funnel: Belief, Trust, Usability

Everything from Package 1, Plus:

  • Impact Story Microsite — Modern, donor-ready storytelling experience with donation integration

Everything from Package 2, Plus:

This is not "content"—it's your anchor story.
