    What's Included

  • 6-Month Social Media Content Calendar — Monthly themes tied to fundraising and programs

    What It Is

    A pre-built planning system that maps your content to your actual fundraising and program cycle—no more scrambling for post ideas.

    What's Included

    • Suggested post types for each week (Monday = success story, Wednesday = BTS, Friday = event reminder)

    • Space to add your specific events, campaigns, and deadlines
    • Platform recommendations (what works best on Facebook vs. Instagram)

    Why It Matters

    Delivery: Editable Google Sheet you can duplicate, customize, and reuse

  • Staff Storytelling Training — 1-hour live webinar on capturing better content

    What It Is

    A live, interactive workshop that teaches your team how to capture better content with just their phones—no fancy gear required.

    What's Included

    • Framing and composition basics (how to make a kid's face tell the story)
    • Lighting tips for indoor club spaces
    • Audio capture techniques (so you can actually hear the interview)
    • Storytelling structure (beginning, middle, end—even in a 30-second video)
    • Hashtag and caption strategy
    • Q&A session tailored to your team's specific challenges

    Why It Matters

    Your club directors will feel confident shooting content instead of intimidated. You'll get more (and better) content from all 5 clubs without hiring a videographer.
